The Business Model

Not a broker. Not a primary insurer. A Reinsurance MGA.

Reinsurers grant Sky Re delegated authority to assess, price, bind and service complex risks, uniting on-the-ground decision-making in Riyadh with local and international reinsurance balance sheets.

01

Our market identity

A pioneering onshore P&C Reinsurance Managing General Agency in Saudi Arabia.

02

Distinct from a primary insurer

Rather than competing with primary insurers, we empower cedants with specialized reinsurance capacity, product innovation and risk assessment.

03

Distinct from a traditional broker

We underwrite on behalf of reinsurers with delegated authority, converting fixed underwriting costs into variable efficiency.

The Sky Re Edge

Three pillars of delegated precision

How we act on behalf of reinsurers: inside a written mandate, with technical depth and disciplined follow-through across the life of every risk.

Pillar 01 · Delegated Mandate

Acting on behalf of reinsurers, with disciplined governance

We underwrite within rigorous technical guidelines and governance frameworks agreed with capacity providers, serving as the trusted onshore custodian of reinsurance capital.

Pillar 02 · Technical Pricing

Specialized risk assessment and tailored structuring

We evaluate complex commercial, industrial and project risks from inside the Saudi market, structuring bespoke covers, including Cyber, PI and Mega Projects, that improve decision-making.

Pillar 03 · Distribution & Post-Bind

Risk pooling, local distribution and post-bind coordination

We facilitate pooling and diversification across local and international capacity, and streamline post-bind coordination and claims technical support for faster resolutions.
