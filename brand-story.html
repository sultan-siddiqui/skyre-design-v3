00 · Prologue

When partnership is complete, a star is born.

This is not just a visual identity. It is the story of two opposing crescents that meet, and from their meeting a new guiding star is born. Bring your cursor to the centre to join them.

Scroll

The story of the symbol

01 / 06

The Form

The mark is formed by two opposing, rising crescents. When they join, the act of joining itself shapes a star. The star is an emergent form: the natural result of a partnership completed.

Reinsurance perspective · sharing risks & rewards

02 / 06

The Upper Crescent Global Market Reach (Offshore)

It represents international capacity, global underwriting networks, offshore capital, and the institutional reach that Sky Re brings into the Kingdom.

03 / 06

The Downward Crescent Local Authority (Onshore) & Protection Canopy

It represents the onshore side of the partnership and carries four essential meanings:

  1. Regional insight
  2. Proactive regulatory engagement
  3. Precision in underwriting
  4. Protective structure for the local market
04 / 06

The Moment of Joining

The most important event in the mark is not either crescent alone, but the precise moment they join. That moment is the partnership becoming real. Everything that matters, the star, the guidance and the completed structure, is born from the join.

05 / 06

The Emergent Star Sky Re

At the exact moment the crescents join, the partnership itself gives rise to a new guiding star: Sky Re. Not a separately designed element, but the visible result of a successful union between Global Market Reach and Local Authority & Protection.

06 / 06

The Optical Pathway

The eye travels from the outer crescents inward to the star, then expands again, mirroring the flow of capacity: international reach and local authority meet at the centre, and from that meeting, guidance and underwriting solutions flow outward to the market.

03 · Core Philosophy

Core philosophy

Sky Re does not stand outside the partnership. It is the living result of the partnership being completed.

The joining of Global Market Reach with Local Authority and Protection naturally creates Sky Re: the new guiding star that emerges from a partnership of sharing risks and rewards.

Sky Re Symbolism · Final & Locked

04 · Deeper & Subtle Dimensions

Beyond the first reading, layers that enrich the brand

01

The Act of Joining as the Critical Moment

What matters most is not the individual crescents, but the precise moment they join: the partnership becoming real. Everything is born from the join.

02

Protection Canopy + Elevation

The downward crescent shelters the local market while both forms keep rising. Protection that never suppresses growth; elevation that stays rooted in local authority and regulatory care.

03

Sharing of Risks and Rewards

The crescents face and complete each other: mutual exposure, mutual benefit. Neither side stands alone; the very definition of true partnership in reinsurance.

04

Emergent Guidance

The star does not impose itself from outside; it appears only when the partnership is complete. Genuine leadership arises from successful joining, not from solitary ambition.

05

Optical Pathway

From the edges inward to the star, then outward again: the operational flow of capacity. Reach and authority meet at the centre, and solutions flow out to the market.

06

Pattern of Restraint

No excess decoration, no forced closure into a full circle, no aggressive collision. The discipline of the geometry mirrors the discipline of underwriting and regulatory engagement.

05 · Logo Philosophy

Crescents, star and horizon, within a balanced square

Every curve of the mark is drawn from circle centres placed on a single diagonal: the horizon axis that runs from corner to corner of the square.

  3. 03
